04 — How it goes

Four moves,
no mystery

Roughly eight to eleven weeks from first call to launch day. You will know exactly where the project is every Friday.

Step 01 · Week 1

Interrogate

We take the business apart before we draw anything. Who buys, why they hesitate, and what the site has to do that a phone call cannot.

  • Kickoff and stakeholder interviews
  • Audience, competitor and category teardown
  • Content audit and messaging spine
  • Success metrics agreed in writing
Step 02 · Weeks 2–3

Draw

Art direction before pixels. You see two genuine directions — not one idea and two decoys — then we take the winner all the way down.

  • Two art directions, presented live
  • Full desktop and mobile design
  • Motion prototype for the key moments
  • Copy pass alongside the layout
Step 03 · Weeks 4–8

Build

Componentised, typed and tested. Staging goes up in week four and stays up, so nobody is reviewing screenshots of a website.

  • Component library and design tokens
  • CMS modelling and editor wiring
  • Accessibility and performance passes
  • Staging review every Friday
Step 04 · Launch

Ship & hold

Launch is a date, not an event. We deploy, watch the numbers for a month, and fix anything that moves the wrong way — no invoice.

  • Deploy, DNS, analytics and search console
  • Editor training and a written handbook
  • Thirty days of fixes included
  • Optional monthly care plan
