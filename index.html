How it goes

Four moves,
no mystery

04
Step 01 · Week 1

Interrogate

We take the business apart before drawing anything — who buys, why they hesitate, what the site has to do that a phone call can't.

  • Kickoff and stakeholder interviews
  • Audience & competitor teardown
  • Success metrics agreed in writing
Step 02 · Weeks 2–3

Draw

Art direction before pixels. Two genuine directions, not one idea and a decoy, then the winner goes all the way down.

  • Two art directions, presented live
  • Full desktop and mobile design
  • Motion prototype for key moments
Step 03 · Weeks 4–8

Build

Componentised, typed and tested. Staging goes up in week four and stays up — nobody reviews screenshots of a website here.

  • Component library and tokens
  • Accessibility and performance passes
  • Staging review every Friday
Step 04 · Launch

Ship & hold

Launch is a date, not an event. We watch the numbers for a month and fix anything that moves the wrong way, no invoice.

  • Deploy, DNS, analytics
  • Editor training & handbook
  • Thirty days of fixes included
